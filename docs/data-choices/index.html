Local kitchen data

Kitchen Prep Board stores ordinary boards, tasks, quantities, templates, notes, timers and preferences in app-private storage on your Android device. GoodUse Studios does not operate a cloud database for this content.

Delete local data

Use Settings → Privacy & legal → Delete local data to remove app-owned local boards, tasks, templates, timer records, locally learned duration history and local preferences covered by the delete function. You can also clear app storage or uninstall the app.

Deleting local app data does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage/cancel subscriptions separately through Google Play.

Google advertising privacy choices

Where required, Google UMP provides the privacy/consent interface used by the app's Google advertising. When Google reports that a privacy-options form is required or available, Kitchen Prep Board exposes it from Settings.

Google-controlled advertising information is subject to Google's own privacy controls, policies and retention practices.

Support-data requests

If you want to request access, correction or deletion of support information held directly by GoodUse Studios, email lrodeveloperr@gmail.com. Because core kitchen data stays on your device, GoodUse Studios ordinarily cannot access or retrieve it for you.
