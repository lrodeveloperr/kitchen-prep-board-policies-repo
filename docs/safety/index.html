Kitchen Prep Board is an organizational aid only.

Do not treat a timer, task-completion state, suggested duration or schedule as proof that food is safe or fully cooked.

Always use appropriate safety controls

  • Follow applicable food-safety laws, health-code requirements and workplace procedures.
  • Use a suitable calibrated thermometer where internal temperature matters.
  • Follow manufacturer and workplace instructions for cooking, chilling, cooling, holding and reheating.
  • Manage allergens and cross-contamination using appropriate procedures independent of the app.
  • Verify quantities, dates and timing before relying on a board.

Timer reliability

Timer alerts can be affected by device settings, notification permissions, exact-alarm availability, operating-system restrictions, battery management, reboot, clock changes or hardware/software failure. Keep appropriate independent kitchen controls where a missed alert could create risk.

Responsibility

You remain responsible for food-safety and operational decisions. Kitchen Prep Board does not certify food safety, professional competence or legal compliance.
