At a glance

Kitchen Prep Board is local-first and does not require an account. Ordinary kitchen-board content stays on your device. The paid iOS app has no advertising, analytics, cloud sync or in-app purchases. The free Android app uses Google Mobile Ads, Google UMP manages advertising privacy choices where required, and Google Play handles the optional remove-ads subscription.

2. Information stored locally

The app may store board names, tasks, pasted task text, quantities, units, on-hand values, prep-gap calculations, templates, notes, reference URLs, timing objectives, timer state, task status, preferences and locally learned duration observations in app-private storage on your device. GoodUse Studios does not operate a cloud database that receives this content merely because you use the app.

3. iOS

The iOS app is paid upfront and contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account, cloud sync, subscription or in-app purchase. It does not track users or request App Tracking Transparency permission. Optional local notifications are used only for timers created by the user. Based on the shipped iOS data flow, the App Store privacy response is Data Not Collected.

4. Android advertising and purchases

The free Android app uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob. Google may process information such as IP address, general location, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device or advertising identifiers where available and permitted. Where required, Google UMP presents consent or privacy-choice interfaces. Google Play Billing handles the optional monthly remove-ads subscription; GoodUse Studios does not receive your full payment-card number.

5. Support communications

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots or diagnostic details. Ordinary support does not require your kitchen-board content. Our normal retention period for support communications is 24 months after the matter is closed or last substantively active, unless longer retention is reasonably required for a dispute, security incident, fraud prevention, tax/accounting requirement, legal claim or legal obligation.

6. Diagnostics

The app does not include Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. Google Play may provide Android crash and ANR information through Android vitals where users/devices participate in Google/Android diagnostics. The iOS app does not include a developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

7. Permissions and device capabilities

The app may use notifications for timer alerts, keep-awake behavior and operating-system time/time-zone signals for timer recovery. Android also uses Internet access for Google advertising, UMP and Play services. Optional permissions are requested in context. Denying notifications may reduce timer-alert reliability but does not prevent ordinary local board creation.

8. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to platform behavior. Settings includes a Delete local data action for app-owned boards, tasks, templates, timers, locally learned duration history and local preferences. Deleting local data does not cancel an Android Google Play subscription.

11. Audience and safety

Kitchen Prep Board is intended and marketed for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. It is an organizational aid: timers, completion indicators and scheduling do not prove food safety, doneness, safe internal temperature, allergen control, storage safety or freedom from cross-contamination.

12. Security

We use a local-first architecture to reduce unnecessary transmission of kitchen-board data. App-private storage and encrypted platform connections reduce risk, but no security measure can guarantee absolute security.
