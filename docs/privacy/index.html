At a glance

Kitchen Prep Board is local-first. You do not need an account. Ordinary kitchen-board content stays on your device. The free Android version uses Google Mobile Ads, Google UMP manages advertising privacy choices where required, and Google Play handles the optional remove-ads subscription.

1. Provider and contact

2. Information stored locally

The app may store board names, tasks, pasted/shared task text, quantities, units, on-hand values, prep-gap calculations, templates, notes, reference URLs, timing objectives, timer state, task status, preferences and locally learned duration observations in app-private storage on your device. GoodUse Studios does not operate a cloud database that receives this content merely because you use the app.

3. Advertising

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob. Google may process information such as IP address (which may indicate general location), app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device or advertising identifiers where available and permitted. Google may use this information for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention under its own policies.

Where required, Google UMP presents consent or privacy-choice interfaces before advertising data is used in ways requiring consent. Depending on your region and choices, the app may request personalized, non-personalized or limited advertising, or may delay an ad request until permitted. Applicable Google privacy choices can be reopened from Settings.

Kitchen Prep Board Android 1.0 is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The app does not collect or store a date of birth and does not implement a child/teen advertising branch.

4. Google Play purchases

Google Play Billing handles the optional monthly remove-ads subscription. GoodUse Studios does not receive your full payment-card number. The app receives purchase/entitlement information needed to determine whether ads should be shown. Google Play's terms and privacy policy apply to the payment transaction.

5. Support communications

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots or diagnostic details. Ordinary support does not require your kitchen-board content.

Our normal retention period for support communications is 24 months after the matter is closed or last substantively active, unless a longer period is reasonably required for an unresolved dispute, security incident, fraud prevention, tax/accounting requirement, legal claim or another legal obligation.

6. Crash and ANR diagnostics

Android 1.0 does not include Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. GoodUse Studios may receive crash and ANR information through Google Play Android vitals where users/devices participate in Google/Android diagnostics. If an embedded analytics or crash-reporting SDK is added later, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that release.

7. Permissions and device capabilities

The app may use notifications for timer alerts, Android exact-alarm access where needed and permitted for reliable timer behavior, boot/time/time-zone signals for timer recovery, and Internet access for Google advertising, UMP and Play services. Optional permissions are requested in context. Denying an optional permission may reduce timer-alert reliability but should not prevent ordinary local board creation.

8. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to platform behavior. Android backup is disabled in the current release configuration.

Settings includes a Delete local data action for app-owned boards, tasks, templates, timers, locally learned duration history and local preferences. Deleting local app data does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Google-controlled advertising and payment records are subject to Google's own retention and privacy controls.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, objection/restriction, portability, or opt-out of certain targeted advertising/sale/sharing. Some requests concern information held by Google rather than GoodUse Studios because core kitchen-board data remains local. For information held by GoodUse Studios, contact lrodeveloperr@gmail.com.

Kitchen Prep Board Android 1.0 is intended and marketed for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children.

12. Food-safety boundary

Kitchen Prep Board is an organizational aid. Timers, task completion, duration suggestions and scheduling do not prove food safety, doneness, safe internal temperature, allergen control, storage safety or freedom from cross-contamination. Follow applicable food-safety rules, workplace procedures and competent guidance.
