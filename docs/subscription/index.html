Renewal and cancellation

The subscription renews automatically each month unless cancelled under Google Play's subscription rules. Manage or cancel it through Google Play. Cancelling stops future renewal but normally keeps ad removal active through the already-paid billing period.

Refunds

Refund eligibility is governed by Google Play policy and applicable mandatory consumer law.

Local data

Starting, cancelling or expiring the remove-ads subscription does not delete your local kitchen boards. Deleting local app data does not cancel the subscription.
